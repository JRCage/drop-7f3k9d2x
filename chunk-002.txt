    deduped by trade_id, 2026-11-02T22:00:15Z onward; ts_ms is the
    Coinbase venue trade time converted onto the local clock with a
    measured offset (logger README: residual alignment uncertainty a
    few hundred ms; the VM clock ran ~1 s behind venue clocks and the
    conversion removes that skew). 19.8M BTC prints across 17 daily
    files were reduced to minute buckets for this export.
  [coinbase-candles-1m] Coinbase Exchange public 1-minute candles,
    fetched 2026-11-18, used for 2026-10-29 through the tape start
    (118 markets): the value is the candle close, i.e. the last trade
    of the minute ending at the stated instant.
Conventions:
  btc_price_at_close_minus_30m / _15m = last Coinbase trade AT OR
    BEFORE that instant. If the newest trade was >10 s old (a logger
    gap), the age is printed on the line.
  per-minute rows: minute_start -> last trade INSIDE that minute
    (T-15m .. T+5m around the close). A minute with no recorded trade
    is printed as NO DATA — never interpolated.
